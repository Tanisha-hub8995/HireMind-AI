Percentages

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100.

To calculate x percent of y:
(x / 100) × y

Percentage increase:
((New Value - Original Value) / Original Value) × 100

Percentage decrease:
((Original Value - New Value) / Original Value) × 100

Profit and Loss

Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price

Loss = Cost Price - Selling Price

Profit percentage = (Profit / Cost Price) × 100

Loss percentage = (Loss / Cost Price) × 100

Ratio and Proportion

A ratio compares two quantities.

If a:b = c:d, then a × d = b × c.

Time and Work

If a person completes a work in x days, their one-day work is 1/x.

If two people can complete a work in x and y days respectively, their combined one-day work is:

1/x + 1/y
