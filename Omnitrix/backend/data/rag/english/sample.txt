English Grammar

A noun is a word that represents a person, place, thing, or idea.

A verb expresses an action, occurrence, or state of being.

An adjective describes or modifies a noun or pronoun.

An adverb commonly modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb.

Subject-Verb Agreement

A singular subject generally takes a singular verb.

A plural subject generally takes a plural verb.

Example:
The student works hard.
The students work hard.

Tenses

Present simple tense is commonly used for habits, routines, and general facts.

Example:
She studies every day.

Past simple tense describes an action completed in the past.

Example:
She studied yesterday.

Vocabulary

A synonym is a word with a similar meaning to another word.

An antonym is a word with an opposite meaning.
